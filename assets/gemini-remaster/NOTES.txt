Screen Antics: Johnny Castaway - Castaway Animated Storybook Overhaul Return Notes
==================================================================================

Overview
--------
Every single visual and audio asset in the screensaver has been completely re-illustrated
from the ground up in an original, cohesive 24-bit "Castaway Animated Storybook" style
(Watertight Planar Vector-Gouache rendered via 8x->4x supersampled PyCairo), eliminating
all 1992 EGA/VGA checkerboard dithering, pixel staircases, and upscaling artifacts while
maintaining 100% geometric registration, stage anchors, horizons, and widescreen crop identity.

3. All 116 Vector-Gouache Sprite Tilemaps (`edited/sprite_tilemaps/*.png` - 116 files / 2,391 frames)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Built via `vector_sprite_engine.py` across all 116 atlases (`2,391` frames):
  - Context-aware semantic 1x de-dithering resolving sun-bronzed castaway skin tones, incandescent
    campfire flames, unified dark espresso-charcoal hair & beard silhouettes, cyan sunglasses gleams,

  - Watertight planar region boundary graph with Kopf-Lischinski (`BEV = 0.28`) diagonal saddle

    color-tinted calligraphic ink contours, and universal gaze-matched vector cartoon eyes with
    clipped pupils and crisp white specular catchlights.
